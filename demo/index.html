Built for the people reading the chart.

Every chart gets these by default. There's nothing to configure.

Measure any change

Hold Shift and drag between two points to see the difference and the percentage change for every series. On a phone, long-press and then drag.

Reads right to left

On a right-to-left page, time runs from the right and the values sit on the right, the way Arabic readers expect. Labels, tooltips and the legend follow.

Works with a keyboard and a screen reader

Press Tab to reach the chart, then use the arrow keys. Each value is read aloud, a summary describes the whole chart, and a hidden table holds the data. Press ♪ to hear the trend.

The screen reader's words will appear here.

Follows your theme

Charts take their colours from your page's text colour. On dark pages they switch to a palette tuned for dark backgrounds. Both palettes pass a colour-blindness check.

100,000 points, no waiting

Dense data is reduced to the highest and lowest value in each pixel column, so the shape stays exact while drawing stays fast.

Add it to your page.

One script tag. Then point it at an element.

<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/nahsamahammed106-gif/minichart@main/src/minichart.js"></script>
<div id="chart"></div>
<script>
  const chart = MiniChart('#chart', {
    type: 'line',            // 'line', 'area' or 'bar'
    title: 'Visitors',
    labels: ['Jan', 'Feb', 'Mar'],
    series: [{ name: 'Visits', data: [120, 340, 280] }],
  });
</script>

Options, interactions and styling are in the README.
